OUR STORY

From Industrial Heritage to Solar Engineering

SolStar Radiance was established to bring the same engineering discipline and business credibility of the Ali Embroidery Mills group into Pakistan's growing solar energy sector.

We work with homeowners, commercial businesses and industrial facilities to design and install solar systems that are engineered for the site, not just sold off a catalog — from energy audit and system design through installation, net metering approvals and ongoing support.

Our approach centers on transparent specifications, quality components, and a responsive after-sales team, so clients know exactly what they're getting and can count on support long after commissioning.
